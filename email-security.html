E-mail security

Make it much harder for fraudsters to send e-mail in your name, and help more of your real e-mail reach the inbox. We set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC and tighten them in safe steps.

E-mail us about this

What you get

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for your domain, for your e-mail provider and for every service that sends e-mail in your name.
  • Tightened in safe steps, from watching to quarantine to reject. At each step we check that your real e-mail still gets through, and you get a short report.
  • Optional monitoring: each month we read your DMARC reports and tell you if anything changes.

How it works

  1. Tell us by e-mail what you need. We reply with what's included and a fixed price.
  2. If we need access, you give it once.
  3. We set it up, test it and send you a short guide.

Good to know

No setup can promise that every e-mail lands in every inbox. That also depends on what you write and on the receiver's filters.
